Business Problem
Patients may experience long wait times that negatively affect satisfaction and operational performance.
Project Objective

Analyze hospital patient data to identify factors influencing patient satisfaction and operational efficiency, with emphasis on patient wait time as the primary improvement lever.
Goals
- Understand patient demographics.
- Analyze admission trends.
- Measure (and lessen) wait times. 
- Evaluate patient satisfaction. (Increase Patient Satisfaction )
- Identify operational improvement opportunities.
Scope
Analyze Patient Admission Date, Age, Gender, Race, Department Referral, Admission Flag, Satisfaction Score, and Wait Time.
Key Business Questions
- What is the average wait time?
- What is the overall and average satisfaction score?
- Which departments have the longest waits?
- Does longer wait time reduce satisfaction?
- Which patient groups experience the longest waits?
- When are peak admission periods?
- Why does the patient give us a low satisfaction rating?
Primary KPI
Patient Satisfaction Score (Direct Measurement)
Admission Rate 
Primary Lever
Patient Wait Time (Controllable) (Strong operational lever)
Department Referral (Process Lever)
Admission date/time (Staffing Lever)
Supporting Metrics
Admission Rate, Average Wait Time, Average Satisfaction, Patient Volume by Date, Referral Distribution.
